# Print Specifications — Standing Pull-Up Banner (1m x 2m)

## 1. Physical Specifications
* **Finished Dimensions:** 1,000 mm (width) x 2,000 mm (height) (1.0 m x 2.0 m)
* **Bleed Allowance:** 5 mm on top, left, and right; 100 mm at the bottom (cassette roller lead-in)
* **Safe Zone:** Keep all critical text, logos, and QR codes at least 40 mm inside the trim edges, and at least 120 mm above the bottom edge.
* **Cassette Fitting:** Standard 1,000 mm aluminum pull-up banner cassette with top clamp bar and 3-piece bungee support pole.

## 2. Recommended Print Substrates (Joburg Print Shops)
* **Recommended Material:** 440gsm – 510gsm Ultra-Smooth Non-Curl Greyback PVC Banner (prevents edge curling and back-light shine-through) or 220gsm Non-Curl Fabric.
* **Finish:** Matte or Satin (avoids overhead glare from exhibition hall lights).

## 3. Digital File Formats Included
1. **`banner.svg`**: Pure vector SVG format (lossless, infinitely scalable, text vectorized or high-contrast web-safe). Can be imported directly into CorelDraw, Adobe Illustrator, or Onyx RIP.
2. **`banner-print.html`**: Pixel-perfect HTML5/CSS3 print stylesheet configured at `@page { size: 1000mm 2000mm; }`.
3. **`banner-print-highres.pdf`**: Generated vector PDF via headless Chrome engine.

## 4. Key Elements & Placement
* **Top Header (1.7m – 2.0m from ground):** Master branding for Sputnik Tech Group & Sputnik Devs Studio visible across crowded exhibition halls.
* **Middle Section (1.1m – 1.6m from ground):** Eye-level and arm-level placement for Tradey Bay App and Student Residence Management with scannable vector QR codes.
* **Lower Section (0.6m – 1.1m from ground):** Sputnik Devs Academy learnership drive and tech stack badges.
* **Bottom 100mm:** Reserved for the roller cassette mechanism.
