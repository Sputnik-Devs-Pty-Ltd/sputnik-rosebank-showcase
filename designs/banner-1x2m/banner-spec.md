# Print Specifications — Standing Pull-Up Banner (1m x 2m)

## 1. Physical Specifications
* **Finished Dimensions:** 1,000 mm (width) x 2,000 mm (height) (1.0 m x 2.0 m)
* **Bleed Allowance:** 5 mm on top, left, and right; 100 mm at the bottom (cassette roller lead-in)
* **Safe Zone:** Keep all critical text, logos, and QR codes at least 40 mm inside the trim edges, and at least 120 mm above the bottom edge.
* **Cassette Fitting:** Standard 1,000 mm aluminum pull-up banner cassette with top clamp bar and 3-piece bungee support pole.

## 2. Recommended Print Substrates (Joburg Print Shops)
* **Recommended Material:** 440gsm – 510gsm Ultra-Smooth Non-Curl Greyback PVC Banner (prevents edge curling and back-light shine-through) or 220gsm Non-Curl Fabric.
* **Finish:** Matte or Satin (avoids overhead glare from exhibition hall lights).

## 3. Decluttered Visual Architecture & Aesthetic Balance
Following leadership review, the banner was systematically decluttered:
* **Watered-Down Paragraphs:** Removed dense, multi-line paragraph text across product cards in favor of bold, punchy feature chips and high-contrast metrics.
* **Generous Negative Space:** Ample breathing room between sections ensuring legibility from 3–5 meters across a bustling exhibition hall.
* **Harmonized Zaha Hadid Curves:** Balanced parametric fluid purple ribbons (`#7c3aed`, `#8b5cf6`, `#c084fc`, `#3b0764`) that frame the content gracefully without overpowering typography.
* **Light-Theme Cohesion:** Crisp, light studio aesthetic (`#f8fafc` to `#ffffff`) with subtle architectural gridlines.
