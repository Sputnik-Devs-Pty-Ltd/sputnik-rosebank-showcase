# Print Specifications — Staff Showcase T-Shirts (Prince & Kenneth)

## 1. Garment Details
* **Garment Type:** Premium Heavyweight Crewneck T-Shirt
* **Fabric:** 100% Combed Cotton, 180gsm – 200gsm (soft touch, pre-shrunk)
* **Color:** Noir Solid Black
* **Quantity Required:** 2 Units
  * **Unit 1:** For **Prince** (Size: Men's L or XL as needed)
  * **Unit 2:** For **Kenneth** (Size: Men's L or XL as needed)

## 2. Print Technique Recommended for Joburg Printers
* **Primary Recommendation:** Direct-to-Film (DTF) Heat Transfer.
  * *Why DTF:* Full-color fidelity for gradients, razor-sharp edges on QR codes, durable through 50+ washes, no minimum quantity setup fees (cost-effective for 2 shirts).
* **Alternative:** Screen Printing with Discharge Base or High-Resolution Vinyl Plotting.

## 3. Placement & Sizing Coordinates
* **Front Left Chest:**
  * Artwork: Sputnik Emblem & Tech Crew Lockup
  * Dimensions: 80 mm x 45 mm
  * Placement: Centered on left breast (approx. 180 mm below shoulder seam, 80 mm from center placket).
* **Front Right Chest (Personalized):**
  * Shirt 1: `PRINCE` • `TECH OPS & DEV`
  * Shirt 2: `KENNETH` • `COO & PRODUCT`
  * Dimensions: 85 mm x 35 mm
  * Placement: Centered on right breast (mirrored height to left chest).
* **Back Center (A3 Full Back Print):**
  * Artwork: "BUILDING AFRICA'S DIGITAL FUTURE" + Scannable Dev Academy QR + Tech Stack Badges.
  * Dimensions: 290 mm width x 400 mm height (Standard A3 print area).
  * Placement: Top edge positioned 60 mm below the back collar seam.
