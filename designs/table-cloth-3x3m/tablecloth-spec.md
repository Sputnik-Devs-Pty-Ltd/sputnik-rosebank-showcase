# Print Specifications — Exhibition Table Cloth (3m x 3m)

## 1. Physical Specifications
* **Finished Dimensions:** 3,000 mm x 3,000 mm (3.0 m x 3.0 m square drape/throw)
* **Table Context:** Designed for standard South African 1.8m exhibition tables (1,800 mm width x 750 mm depth x 740 mm height).
* **Hem / Finishing:** Double-stitched 15 mm perimeter hem with safety margins to prevent fraying during multi-day showcase use.
* **Aesthetic Theme:** Light-theme Zaha Hadid architectural parametric fluid curves & contour streamlines in royal brand purples (`#3b0764`, `#581c87`, `#7c3aed`, `#8b5cf6`, `#a855f7`).

## 2. Decluttered Architectural Layout & Branding Architecture
Following executive feedback, the tablecloth was redesigned to eliminate crowded architectural flowcharts, paragraphs, and multi-column elevator pitch notes. The current production design emphasizes premium brand presence, clean product identity, and effortless QR scanability:

1. **Zaha Hadid Fluid Ribbon Canvas:**
   * Sweeping organic curves and parametric streamlines arc across all 4 drape skirts and tabletop zones.
   * Creates an unforgettable visual atmosphere from anywhere in the exhibition hall while keeping negative space open and clean.

2. **Tabletop Surface (Center 1,800 mm x 750 mm, X: 600–2400, Y: 1125–1875):**
   * **Master Co-Brand Lockup:** Prominent dual logos for **Sputnik Tech Group** and **Sputnik Devs Studio**.
   * **Minimal Laptop & Device Demo Staging Pads:** Dual high-contrast placement reticles ("Live App Interactive Terminal" & "Enterprise Cloud Terminal") leaving the table surface uncluttered for physical laptop displays and marketing collateral.

## 3. Recommended Print Substrates (Joburg Print Shops)
* **Material:** 210gsm – 240gsm Soft Knitted Polyester Fabric / Poly-Drape (Dye-Sublimation Full Color Print).
* **Properties:** Wrinkle-resistant, machine-washable, flame-retardant (standard requirement in SA conference centers).
* **Finish:** Matte non-reflective fabric (ensures smartphone cameras can scan the QR codes under bright convention lighting).
