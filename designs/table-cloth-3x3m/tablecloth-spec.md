# Print Specifications — Exhibition Table Cloth (3m x 3m)

## 1. Physical Specifications
* **Finished Dimensions:** 3,000 mm x 3,000 mm (3.0 m x 3.0 m square drape/throw)
* **Table Context:** Designed for standard South African 1.8m exhibition tables (1,800 mm width x 750 mm depth x 740 mm height).
* **Hem / Finishing:** Double-stitched 10 mm hem all around the outer perimeter to prevent fraying.
* **Layout Zones:**
  * **Tabletop Surface (Center 1800 x 750 mm):** Includes two interactive scanning pads (Tradey Bay app download & Sputnik Devs Academy learnership applications) plus central co-branding crest.
  * **Front Drape (2400 x 700 mm):** Vertical drop facing visitors down the aisle with high-contrast company logos, sub-brands, and mission statement.
  * **Side Drops (600 mm overhang each side):** Vertical logos along left and right skirts.

## 2. Recommended Print Substrates (Joburg Print Shops)
* **Material:** 210gsm – 240gsm Soft Knitted Polyester Fabric / Poly-Drape (Dye-Sublimation Full Color Print).
* **Properties:** Wrinkle-resistant, machine-washable, fire-retardant (ideal for campus exhibition halls).
* **Finish:** Matte non-reflective fabric (ensures QR codes scan cleanly under fluorescent hall lighting).

## 3. Digital File Formats Included
1. **`tablecloth.svg`**: Full vector SVG at exact 1:1 scale (3000 x 3000 mm) with all graphics, QR codes, and typography.
2. **`tablecloth-print.html`**: Print stylesheet ready for direct browser export.
3. **`tablecloth-print-highres.pdf`**: Generated high-DPI PDF.
