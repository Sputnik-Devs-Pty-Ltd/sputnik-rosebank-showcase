# Print Specifications — Exhibition Table Cloth (3m x 3m)

## 1. Physical Specifications
* **Finished Dimensions:** 3,000 mm x 3,000 mm (3.0 m x 3.0 m square drape/throw)
* **Table Context:** Designed for standard South African 1.8m exhibition tables (1,800 mm width x 750 mm depth x 740 mm height).
* **Hem / Finishing:** Double-stitched 15 mm perimeter hem with safety margins to prevent fraying during multi-day showcase use.
* **Aesthetic Theme:** Light-theme Zaha Hadid architectural parametric fluid curves & contour streamlines in royal brand purples (`#3b0764`, `#581c87`, `#7c3aed`, `#8b5cf6`, `#a855f7`).

## 3. Recommended Print Substrates (Joburg Print Shops)
* **Material:** 210gsm – 240gsm Soft Knitted Polyester Fabric / Poly-Drape (Dye-Sublimation Full Color Print).
* **Properties:** Wrinkle-resistant, machine-washable, flame-retardant (standard requirement in SA conference centers).
* **Finish:** Matte non-reflective fabric (ensures smartphone cameras can scan the QR codes under bright convention lighting).
