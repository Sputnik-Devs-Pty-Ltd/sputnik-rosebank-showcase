# Print Specifications — Exhibition Table Cloth (3m x 3m)

## 1. Physical Specifications
* **Finished Dimensions:** 3,000 mm x 3,000 mm (3.0 m x 3.0 m square drape/throw)
* **Table Context:** Designed for standard South African 1.8m exhibition tables (1,800 mm width x 750 mm depth x 740 mm height).
* **Hem / Finishing:** Double-stitched 15 mm perimeter hem with safety margins to prevent fraying during multi-day showcase use.

## 2. 5-Zone Exhibition Surface Architecture
The 3m x 3m canvas is divided into 5 functional operational zones:

1. **Tabletop Interactive Command Console (Center 1,800 mm x 750 mm, X: 600–2400, Y: 1125–1875):**
   * **Left Pod (Tradey Bay Launchpad):** Smartphone placement reticle with live scannable QR code (200x200mm) pointing to Google Play Store, Android Live & iOS badges, and 4 point-and-explain pillars (Zero fees, textbooks/dorm gear, verified student trust, in-app chat).
   * **Center Pod (Dual-Core Architecture Blueprint):** Intricate technical flowchart connecting Campus Clients, Tradey Bay mobile app, Student Residence Management System & Shopnik SaaS web portals, the Unified Sputnik Cloud Engine (.NET 10, Azure, PostgreSQL, Redis), and the Sputnik Devs Academy talent pipeline.
   * **Right Pod (Devs Academy & SaaS Testbed):** Interactive scan reticle with scannable QR code pointing to the WIL software learnership application portal, plus secondary link for the Student Residence Management System.
2. **Front Aisle Billboard (2,600 mm x 800 mm, Y: 1900–2750):**
   * Hangs vertically facing visitors walking down the exhibition hall aisle.
   * Dual-split cyber layout:
     * **Left Half:** Sputnik Tech Group & Tradey Bay mobile app features, verified student community badges, and Google Play install callouts.
     * **Right Half:** Sputnik Devs Studio, Student Residence Management System, Shopnik SaaS, and Devs Academy WIL learnership hiring banners.
   * Bottom panoramic runner with portal URLs and staff interaction callouts.
3. **Left Side Skirt (700 mm x 440 mm, X: 50–550, Y: 1100–1900):**
   * Facing left aisle foot traffic. 100% focused on Sputnik Tech Group and Tradey Bay with a 180mm scannable QR code and Google Play badges.
4. **Right Side Skirt (700 mm x 440 mm, X: 2450–2950, Y: 1100–1900):**
   * Facing right aisle foot traffic. 100% focused on Sputnik Devs Studio and Devs Academy WIL learnerships with a 180mm scannable QR code.
5. **Rear Operator Tactical HUD (1,700 mm x 680 mm, X: 600–2400, Y: 350–1100):**
   * Hanging down behind the table, rotated 180° so it reads right-side-up to **Prince** and **Kenneth** standing/sitting behind the booth.
   * Provides 3 columns:
     * *Column 1:* Student 30-Second Elevator Pitch & conversation hooks.
     * *Column 2:* Residence Manager & Faculty 30-Second Pitch for SRMS and The University Hub.
     * *Column 3:* Booth Rapid Metrics, staff assignments, lead capture checklist, and emergency Wi-Fi hotspot details.

## 3. Recommended Print Substrates (Joburg Print Shops)
* **Material:** 210gsm – 240gsm Soft Knitted Polyester Fabric / Poly-Drape (Dye-Sublimation Full Color Print).
* **Properties:** Wrinkle-resistant, machine-washable, flame-retardant (standard requirement in SA conference centers).
* **Finish:** Matte non-reflective fabric (ensures smartphone cameras can scan the QR codes under bright halogen or fluorescent convention lighting).
