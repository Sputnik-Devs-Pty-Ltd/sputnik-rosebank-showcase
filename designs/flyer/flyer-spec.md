# Print Specifications — Handheld Ecosystem Flyer (A5 Portrait: 148mm x 210mm)

## 1. Physical & Print Specifications
* **Trim Dimensions:** 148 mm (width) x 210 mm (height) — Standard ISO A5 Portrait.
* **Aspect Ratio:** 1 : 1.414 (√2 ISO 216 paper standard).
* **Color Space:** CMYK Full Color (High-fidelity digital / offset litho print reproduction).
* **Safety & Margin Clearance:** 4 mm internal safety margin around all critical text and scannable QR reticles.
* **Aesthetic Language:** Light-theme Zaha Hadid architectural parametric fluid ribbons and streamlines rendered in official royal brand purples (`#3b0764`, `#581c87`, `#7c3aed`, `#8b5cf6`, `#c084fc`, `#ede9fe`).

---

## 2. Recommended Paper Stocks & Finishes (Johannesburg Print Shops)
* **Premium Handheld Flyer (Recommended):**
  * **Stock:** 170 gsm – 250 gsm High-Bulk Coated Art Paper (Silk / Satin or Soft Matt finish).
  * **Rationale:** Substantial hand feel that communicates corporate and enterprise software credibility without folding or flopping in attendees' hands.
  * **Coating:** Matt lamination or satin dispersion varnish (non-reflective to ensure effortless smartphone QR code scanning under indoor exhibition lighting).
* **Mass Distribution Alternative:**
  * **Stock:** 135 gsm – 150 gsm Gloss/Silk Art Paper.
  * **Rationale:** Cost-efficient high-volume run for wider student campus distribution.
