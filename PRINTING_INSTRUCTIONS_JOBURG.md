## 4. Pre-Flight Inspection: Testing Before You Pay!

Before leaving the print shop:
1. **Pull open the banner:** Make sure it pulls smoothly from the cassette and stands straight on its pole.
2. **Scan EVERY QR code with your phone camera:**
   * Test the **Tradey Bay** QR: It must open the Google Play Store link immediately!
   * Test the **Academy Apply** QR: It must open `sputnikdevs.com/academy/apply`!
   * Test the **Student Res** QR: It must open `sputnikdevs.com/products/hostel`!
3. **Inspect the table cloth:** Verify the center tabletops scan pads are crisp and text is legible.
4. **Inspect the t-shirt prints:** Rub fingers over the DTF transfer to ensure it was properly heat-pressed and won't peel.
